# Generated Product Images — Batch A

Generation mode: OpenAI built-in `image_gen` tool, one original generation per product.

## Shared prompt

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: production ecommerce catalogue card image for a premium personalized-gift website
Primary request: create an original high-end ecommerce studio photograph of the specified single product.
Scene/backdrop: warm ivory #FFF9F2 seamless backdrop with one pale blush #FBE8E7 architectural arch and plinth; a restrained soft-rose #EBC9C8 secondary plane; no environment or room.
Style/medium: photorealistic premium product photography, refined contemporary Indian gifting aesthetic, original composition.
Composition/framing: 4:3 landscape, centered single product, slight three-quarter camera angle, consistent medium scale, generous clean padding, entire product fully inside safe edges, card-safe crop.
Lighting/mood: controlled diffused softbox lighting, warm but neutral, elegant and calm, soft contact shadow with no harsh contrast.
Color palette: warm ivory, pale blush, soft rose, natural wood, tiny muted-gold #C69B57 hardware accents; any black appears only where intrinsic to the product.
Materials/textures: realistic wood grain, metal, acrylic, fabric and paper detail as applicable; crisp edges and believable depth.
Text: none.
Constraints: one product only; no page UI, logo, watermark, readable words, names, dates, numbers, labels or monograms; no live hands or people; a generic couple may appear only as engraved, printed or illustrated artwork intrinsic to the specified product; no collage layout; no scrapbook styling; no torn paper; no handwriting; no Polaroid; no unrelated props; product fully visible and centered; bright light background only.
Avoid: black or dark background, dramatic low-key lighting, busy decor, flowers unless explicitly part of the specified product, extra gift boxes, brand marks, duplicate objects, cropped product edges.
```

Each asset used the shared prompt followed by its product-specific subject line below.
